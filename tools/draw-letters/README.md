# Step by Draw letter drawings: the build tools

Not published (only `public/` is). These scripts write a letter drawing as code, check it and add it to the app page.

- `kit.py`: the drawing kit (lines, curves, colours, the stepby/1 format).
- `build_<name>.py`: one drawing each. Writes `out/<name>.json` (the drawing), `out/<name>.place.json` (set, slot, Make it move kind, age) and sometimes `out/<name>.white.json` (holes meant to stay white).
- `check.py out/<name>.json`: the line and colour checks (9 checks; every one must pass).
- `sheet.py out/<name>.json out/<name>-steps.png` draws the numbered step sheet; add `--pair` for lines beside the finished picture.
- `apply.py ../../public/draw/index.html name1 name2 ...`: adds built drawings to the app page (data and card, level, Make it move kind, set slot). Stops if a key or slot is already taken.
- `famkit.py`: extra helpers for the family drawings (16 Nov 2026): a peach pencil, circle and arm helpers, and a curve offset for wide ribbons.
- `reapply.py ../../public/draw/index.html name1 ...`: puts a rebuilt drawing back into the app page in place of the old one (same key).
- `carkit.py` and `build_racecars.py` (19 Nov 2026): the Vehicles 1 to 9 race cars (`car1` ... `car9`). The cars were sketched on a 200-wide page and `carkit.T` moves them onto the app's paper, so every car is the same size and sits on the same road. Live from 19 Nov 2026: 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 7 and 9 (4 is for Jet car added at 09:3x). Its white stripe is a hole meant to stay white (`out/car4.white.json`), so the app checker lists it as a gap, like other drawings with white bits. Live from 23 Nov 2026: 3 is for Rocket car (`car3`, facing left), from his own picture of a car with two jets on its flat back: the 3 is the two rockets and their fire. Each bump of the 3 is cut into five pieces where the rocket's end line and the flames meet it, so every join is an exact shared point. Its body and wing are not coloured (white paper), so there is no hole and no white file. Also live from 23 Nov 2026: 8 is for Bubble car (`car8`, facing right), from his own sketch of an 8 standing at the back of a car: the top loop is the glass bubble with the driver in it and the bottom loop is the big back wheel. The 8 is one line (as in `build_num_eggs.py`) and each loop is cut where the car's top line, the floor and the pipe's two lines meet it. Its white stripe is a hole meant to stay white (`out/car8.white.json`), like car4's. All nine are now live. Later on 23 Nov 2026 he asked for all nine to be different colours, so five bodies changed: 1 green, 2 purple, 4 grey, 6 pink, 9 yellow with a red helmet (3 white, 5 orange, 7 blue and 8 red stay). 4 has 12 steps now, not 13: the car, the wing and the wheel middles are coloured grey in one step.
- `apptest.py ../../public <port> out/app/<prefix> key1 key2 ...`: opens the app in headless Chrome (phone and Chromebook size) and goes through every step of each drawing.
- `fkit.py` (22 Nov 2026): for drawings where shapes sit in front of each other (the nine Food and drink drawings: orange, broadbeans, loaf, tomatoes, pumpkin, jasminetea, quiche, raspberries, fruit). A `Net` holds named lines; `node()` cuts every line where it meets another and makes both share the very same point, `hide()` takes away the parts behind a shape in front, and `fill()` walks round the drawn lines that close in a point (like a paint bucket), so each colour edge is made of the very same pieces as the lines.
- `qcheck.py out/<name>.json`: the same 9 checks as `check.py`, much quicker on drawings with many lines (it skips pairs of lines that are far apart when looking for crossings).
- `vkit.py` (22 Nov 2026): a painter's layer over `fkit`, for the five Vehicles capitals built from Claude's sketches (oiltanker, ketch, nuclearsub, yacht, vikingboat). A `Pic` takes its parts back to front: `shape()` is a closed part that hides the lines of everything behind it, `stroke()` is a line that hides nothing (`deco=True` for a line that is only decoration), `region()` hides without drawing. `build()` cuts and trims; `step(k)` gives a step's lines; `ring()` colours a whole part and `face()` colours the space round a point, with `holes` for parts that sit inside the colour. The drawing is written in its sketch's numbers and the `Pic` moves and scales it onto the paper in one place. Two parts that share a wall must not both draw it: give one of them a `region()` and separate `stroke()` sides (see the top box of the oil tanker's cabin).
- `sheet_phone.py out/<name>.json out/<name>-phone.png`: a tall step sheet that reads well on a phone (the letter in red and the finished picture on top, then every step, two across).
- `bcommon.py` (23 Nov 2026): the few things the seven small-letter Vehicles builds share (excavator, narrowboat, rocket, unicycle, van, xebec, ziplinecar): a cloud, a star, a wavy line, and `finish()`, which saves the drawing, runs the 9 checks and draws a quick look (`out/<name>-look.png`). These seven are written with `vkit` from Claude's sketches. `build_van.py yellow` makes the van with a yellow top instead of a white one (`out/van-yellow.json`, not in the app). The rocket's step 1 draws only the top part of its straight side, so the r has a normal shape; step 2 carries that line on down.
- Seven small-letter Food and drink builds (23 Nov 2026), written with `vkit` from Claude's sketches: blueberry, icedtea, kale, onion, quinoa, sushi, teapot. In the iced tea, step 2 is only the short line that joins the i's dot to its line (his ask); the dot ends up as the straw's top corner. The kale's tall line is drawn to the bottom of the leaf in step 1 and carried on as the stalk in step 3; all its leaf lines are pencil. The sushi's s is two strokes (the left edge of the top roll, then the right edge of the roll under it); the rolls stay white, so the board's colour has two holes meant to stay white (`out/sushi.white.json`) and the app checker lists them as gaps. Two things the app's own checker tests that `check.py` does not: no more than 14 new lines in a step (so the quinoa has 14 dots and the sushi 10 seeds), and every number in a path on the paper, a curve's guide points included (the teapot's handle).
- `look.py build_<name>.py`: a quick look at a build without the checks (writes the drawing and `out/<name>-look.png`). The kale's checks take about four minutes.
- Nine more from Claude's sketches (23 Nov 2026), written with `vkit` and `bcommon.finish()`: the Food and drink capitals avocado (A), drumstick (D), hotdog (H), kingprawn (K), nachos (N) and salad (S), and the small-letter Vehicles golfcart (g), mailvan (m) and offroader (o). The golf cart's headlight (the round of the g) is a nearly full circle that meets the front edge at two points: a full circle that only touches the stick fails "no line runs along another". The mail van's m is 38 wide and 21 tall (he asked for it to be less tall than in the sketch). The off-roader is written level and facing right, and its `F()` turns it to face left with its nose up.
- Eight Food and drink numbers (23 Nov 2026), the first drawings in `house-09`, written with `vkit` from Claude's sketches: `build_num_chips.py` (1), `build_num_toast.py` (2), `build_num_pretzel.py` (3), `build_num_plum.py` (5), `build_num_cherry.py` (6), `build_num_popcorn.py` (7), `build_num_eggs.py` (8) and `build_num_liquorice.py` (9). Number 4 came a day later (`build_num_pineapple.py`, below). They share `ncommon.py`: the set and tags, a dot helper, and a `finish()` like `bcommon`'s that also prints how far the drawing reaches on the paper. The toast's jam is two lines, one each side of the cut, so nothing crosses the 2. The 8 (two egg yolks) and the 9 (the wheel and its loose end) are each ONE line, as the number is written: two rings that touch, or a stick that touches a big loop, fail "no line runs along another". The fried eggs' white is a hole meant to stay white (`out/num8eggs.white.json`), so the app checker lists it as a gap. The pretzel's dough is one band: its two edges are smoothed once and cut into parts, so the arms, the loops and the 3 share their end points; the first arm is in front of the second, and both tips are in front of the left side. `check.py` takes from half a minute (chips) to four and a half minutes (pretzel) on these.
- Two more from Claude's sketches (23 Nov 2026), written with `vkit` and `bcommon.finish()`: the small-letter Vehicles snowmobile (s) and the small-letter Food and drink dimsum (d). The snowmobile's s is his own plan: it sits at the front of the track (the bottom of the body, the inside of the front arm, the top of the track, its round front end and its bottom), made of level lines and two half circles. The seat sits on the body's top line (one shared line). The dim sum's d is the bun and the left edge of a chopstick: the bun meets the chopstick at two points (like the golf cart's headlight), and step 1 draws a stick twice as tall as the round, which step 3 carries on up to the top of the chopstick.
- Two more small-letter Food and drink drawings from Claude's sketches (23 Nov 2026), written with `vkit` and `bcommon.finish()`: nut (n) and waffle (w). The nut's n is the green cap of a long narrow nut hanging from a branch (the app calls it a nut, not an acorn: it is in the food set). The waffle's w is his own plan: the waffle's outside edge (left side, bottom, right side) and a line up the middle between the holes. Step 1 draws the w with its three lines the same height; step 2 carries the middle line on up to the top edge (his ask) and closes the top. The twelve holes have thinner lines (1.2). `build_waffle.py yellow` makes a yellow waffle instead of a light brown one (`out/waffle-yellow.json`, not in the app). The waffle's checks take about 75 seconds.
- Three more Food and drink drawings from Claude's sketches (23 Nov 2026), written with `vkit` and `bcommon.finish()`: udon (small u), cake (capital U, an upside-down cake with custard) and eclair (capital E). The udon's u is the bowl itself; its noodles are three solid loops standing on the rim (a `region()` each, so the loop in front hides the ones behind) and they stay white. The cake's U is custard running down the front of the slice: the custard's outline starts and ends on the two tops of the U. The jug is added after the two lines of the falling custard, so its lip is not hidden by their `region()` (the other order fails "no colour goes over another"). The eclair's E is the flat cut end of a piece and its three layer lines (the top of the lid, the top of the cream, the bottom of the base); the whole eclair behind sits to the right so none of its lines stop on the E, and the cream stays white. Both u drawings were sketched for small u and only one fits, so the cake has capital U (the u has no stick, so the shape is the same). Their checks take from half a minute (udon) to a minute and a half (cake).
- Two more Food and drink drawings from Claude's sketches (24 Nov 2026), written with `vkit` and `bcommon.finish()`: yoghurt (small y) and xiaolongbao (capital X). The yoghurt's y is the three middle lines of the mint leaves. Each leaf is a `region()` with its two edges as separate `stroke()`s, and it starts a little way along its line (a short stalk), so only the y's own lines meet at its join. The rim and the yoghurt are plain paper, so nothing white is shut inside a colour. The bowl's curve leaves the rim leaning out a little: a curve that sets off along the oval fails "no line runs along another". The X is where four dumplings meet (his idea): each of its lines is one dumpling's edge carrying straight on as the front dumpling's edge, and the front dumpling's top stands in the notch above the crossing. The back dumpling stands up in front of the rim, so neither the brown inside nor the rim is a ring round the white dumplings. A band on the basket that meets its side exactly at the end of its oval touches the side twice and breaks the colour walk, so each band stops 4 degrees short and meets the side at a slant. Checks take about a minute (yoghurt) and six and a half minutes (xiaolongbao; `qcheck.py` is no quicker for it).
- One more Vehicles drawing from Claude's sketches (24 Nov 2026), written with `vkit` and `bcommon.finish()`: x15 (capital X, the X-15 rocket plane seen from above). Two edges of a solid plane never truly cross, so the X is the plane's back corner, where four real edges meet: the back edge of the near tail wing carries straight on as the end of the body, and the side of the body carries straight on as the side of the fire. Step 1 draws that X; the body's side and the far tail wing's back edge are then carried on from its ends. Nothing overlaps in this view, so there is no `region()` and nothing is hidden: every part is a line that starts and ends on another line. The fire leans (its far edge turns in at the corner), so the same corner on the far side is not a second X. The plane is written along and across its own body and turned onto the paper by `M()`. Its checks take half a minute.
- One more Food and drink capital from Claude's sketches (24 Nov 2026), written with `vkit` and `bcommon.finish()`: zander (capital Z, a whole grilled zander on a long dish with tomatoes, lemon and parsley). The Z is three real edges of the fish: its back, the cut and its belly. Nothing sits on top of anything else, so no part is hidden and the fish needs no `region()`: the head is one line from the Z's top right corner round to its end, the tail part is one line from its start round to its bottom left corner, and the cut splits the fish's colour in two. The eye and the middle of the dish are holes meant to stay white (`out/zander.white.json`), so the app checker lists them as gaps. The lemon step has 14 lines, the most the app allows in a step. `build_zander.py brown` makes a brown tail fin instead of an orange one (`out/zander-brown.json`, not in the app). Its checks take about three and a half minutes.

- One more small-letter Vehicles drawing from Claude's sketches (24 Nov 2026), written with `vkit` and `bcommon.finish()`: yellowmotorbike (small y, a yellow sports motorbike). The y is the front of the seat and the body under it: its two arms are the back of the tank and the front of the seat cowl, and its stick carries on down as the frame. The wheels are plain (tyre and round middle, no spokes); the front mudguard sits on the tyre and the fork is two lines up to the nose. The exhaust only covers the top of the back tyre. - The ninth Food and drink number (24 Nov 2026), written with `vkit` and `ncommon.finish()` from Claude's sketches: `build_num_pineapple.py` (4), so `house-09` is complete. The 4 is the pointed-top kind, as on the Jet car: its slanted line and the top of its straight line are the front leaf of the crown, its flat line is the top of the fruit, and the bottom of its straight line is the upright edge of a little leaf hanging over the fruit. The layered leaves are his idea, and he asked for the three hanging leaves to be the same size (10 wide, 13 tall, 13 apart in the sketch's numbers). Every crown leaf stands on the flat line, so each is a `region()` and one `stroke()` and none draws a bottom edge. The diamond lines are thin (1.2) and are worked out by the script: each runs from the fruit's edge to the fruit's edge or to the first little leaf it reaches and is not carried on past that leaf, and the script stops if one would end on the 4. They cross each other, so `finish()` is called with `allow=('7',)`; with nothing allowed, the only crossings `check.py` finds are between steps 6 and 7. `look.py` does not cover the `ncommon` builds (it only swaps `bcommon.finish`). Its checks take about 45 seconds.
To add a new drawing:

```
mkdir -p out/app
python3 build_<name>.py && python3 check.py out/<name>.json
python3 sheet.py out/<name>.json out/<name>-pair.png --pair
python3 apply.py ../../public/draw/index.html <name>
python3 apptest.py ../../public 8801 out/app/after <key>
```

Rules the drawings follow: the letter is drawn first and whole, as it is written, with a normal letter shape (not stretched wide, tall or flat: if the picture needs longer lines, step 1 draws the first part and a later step carries them on); nothing crosses it (lines may only touch it); every colour edge is on a drawn line; no colour goes over another colour; one pencil a step; all the line steps come before the colour steps.

Needs Python 3 with numpy, scipy, scikit-image and playwright, and Chrome at `/usr/bin/google-chrome`.

Step 1 fixes (18 Nov 2026): 16 live drawings had their step 1 cut so the letter looks natural, with the finished picture unchanged (see `step1fix.py`). If one of them is rebuilt from its build script (tap, toiletroll, electrictrain, num_swan, num_shark, num_pig), run `python3 step1fix.py out/<name>.json` before `apply.py` or `reapply.py`.
